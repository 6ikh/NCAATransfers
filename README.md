# NCAATransfers

## Problem Statement
For skill position players who entered the NCAA transfer portal in the 2021–2025 cycles with immediate eligibility and transferred from a non-power conference school to a power conference school, how does their role and efficiency change from their last season at the old school to their first at the new one?

The transfer portal has become a big part of college football over the years.
Players can change schools and find different situations that could provide them with more playing time or a better opportunity to compete.
An interesting type of transfer is when a player moves from a non-power conference to a power conference school.
A player could have been successful at their old school; moving up to a higher level of competition could affect their role and performance.
We want to examine if skill-position players maintain, improve, or decline in their production and efficiency.
We want to compare a player’s previous season with a school before they transferred with their production and role with their new team.
This way we can see the transfer portals effect on these players.


## The Goal
The goal of our app is to show how skill-position players perform after transferring from a non-power conference school to a power conference school. We will compare each player’s last season at their old school to their first season at their new school using statistics such as usage, box score stats, and PPA. This will help determine whether a player’s role and performance improved, declined, or stayed the same after transferring. A main constraint is missing or incomplete data for some players.
